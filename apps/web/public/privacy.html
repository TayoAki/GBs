Hire Shelby is operated by [Company legal name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Hire Shelby web app and service (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. Hire Shelby is in beta, so features and this policy may change; we will tell you about material changes before they take effect.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a hashed password, or the identity details of a passkey or sign-in provider you use.
  • Your content: messages, chat history, files and attachments, bot instructions, memories, routines, artifacts, screenshots and anything else you or your bots create in the Service.
  • Connections: API keys, sign-in tokens and settings for AI models, apps and tools you connect. These are encrypted before they are stored and are never shown back to you in full.
  • Usage and billing records: which models your bots used, token counts, costs, credit balances and subscription status.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device information, and request logs, which we use to run, secure and debug the Service.
  • Payment details: handled by Stripe. We receive a customer reference, subscription and payment status, but never your full card number.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: run your bots and routines, store your work and sync it across your devices.
  • To bill you, apply credits and prevent fraud.
  • To keep the Service secure, enforce our Terms and investigate abuse.
  • To provide support and tell you about changes to the Service.
  • To understand and improve the beta, using usage records and the feedback you send us.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use your content for advertising, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

Who we share it with

We share data only as needed to run the Service:

  • AI model providers. To answer you and do work, your bots send messages, history, instructions, memories, attachments, screenshots and connected-app content to the model provider in use: the one you connect (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI or OpenRouter) or, if you buy credits, the provider we use for credits. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to that processing.
  • Tools your bots use. Web searches and page fetches go to the search and web services those tools rely on. Apps, MCP servers and other tools you connect receive what your bots send them.
  • Optional services you turn on, such as voice (speech-to-text and text-to-speech providers) or a memory service.
  • Stripe for payments, and an email provider for account emails.
  • Hosting. The Service runs on Railway in the United States, which stores the database, files and logs for us.
  • Legal reasons. When the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or us.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Your content and credentials are encrypted at rest by the application, and each account can only read its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and only connect accounts your bots need.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete your account under Settings → Account, which removes your content and connections from the Service. We keep billing and usage records as long as needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and legal obligations. During the beta we may also need to reset or migrate data; keep your own copy of anything important.

Cookies

We use only the cookies and local storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We do not use advertising cookies.
